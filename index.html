GAME GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY

01

Create a Game

Choose how many players will join the game and customize your game settings.

02

Get the Question

The app will randomly give a question for everyone to answer. You can also create and customize your own questions.

03

Answer the Question

Each player secretly types their own answer in the app.

04

Reveal the Answers

Answers will be revealed one at a time without showing who wrote them.

05

Guess the Friend

Read the revealed answer and guess which friend wrote it.

06

Vote

Choose your guess directly in the app. You'll have a limited time to decide before the voting closes.

07

Next Answer

After voting, the next answer will be revealed. Keep guessing and voting until all answers are revealed.

08

Find the Winner

The player with the most correct guesses wins the round!

TIP

Know your friends well! The better you know them, the easier it is to guess who wrote each answer.
